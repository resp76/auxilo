# Naming — Adiutor

Decided September 9, 2026. Working name **Relay** is superseded by **Adiutor** for public release.

## Decision

- **Name:** Adiutor
- **Primary domain:** `adiutor.app` (available at time of decision — register before launch)
- **Meaning:** Latin for *helper / assistant*. Fits a "do-it-all personal assistant" dashboard (tasks, calendars, contacts, notes, projects, connected accounts).
- **Filing classes to secure:** Nice **Class 9** (downloadable software) and **Class 42** (SaaS).

This document records the availability and trademark screening behind the choice. It is research, not legal advice; a formal clearance opinion from a trademark attorney is still required before filing or launch (see Open actions).

### Typo / defensive domains

The two most likely misspellings are already taken and cannot be secured:

- `aduitor.com` — registered (i/u transposition)
- `adjutor.com` — registered ("adjutant" bleed-through)

Available but low-value typos: `adiuter.com`, `aduiter.com`, `aduitorapp.com`, `adjutorapp.com`. Not recommended to buy — app discovery runs through App Store search and Google, not typed URLs. The real confusion risk is `adiutor.co` (below), not typo domains.

## Trademark screening

Sources: TMView (tmdn.org, aggregates 70+ offices incl. USPTO, EUIPO, WIPO), plus Justia/USPTO mirror indexes. Screened September 9, 2026. **Not an official clearance.**

### United States (USPTO) — clear

No live or pending "Adiutor" mark found. Neighboring marks (ADIUM, ADITUM, ADIOS) index normally; ADIUTOR does not appear. The US Class 9 / 42 software lane is open.

### EU / UK — no software conflict

TMView returned 15 "Adiutor" records worldwide. The only live EU/UK registration does **not** cover software:

- **EUIPO 018216683** — owner **DALTAM EL CIBAO S.r.l.** (Trento, Italy). **Figurative** (logo) mark, registered 2020, live to 2030. Nice classes 5, 9, 25, but the actual goods are COVID-era PPE:
  - Class 5: alcohol-based antibacterial hand sanitizer gel
  - Class 9: anti-pollution respiratory masks; disposable laboratory gloves
  - Class 25: medical gowns
  The Class 9 here is protective equipment, **not** computer software. No goods overlap with a productivity app, and it is a figurative (not word) mark.
- **UK00918216683** — the Brexit-cloned UK equivalent of the above. Same PPE goods, same non-overlap.

### Other "Adiutor" products in market (not blocking, but note for SEO)

- **adiutor.co** — school/education-management software, Ghana-based company founded 2021 (Win/Mac/iOS/Android). Different segment and (apparently) Ghana-only registration, but it ranks for the exact name. This, not typo domains, is the main brand-confusion / SEO competitor to out-rank.
- **Kernel Adiutor** — open-source Android kernel-tuning utility. Unrelated; no commercial mark.

## Open actions before launch

1. Register `adiutor.app` (primary) and the two `.com` fallbacks.
2. Obtain a **written trademark clearance opinion** in Classes 9 + 42 from a qualified attorney. The DALTAM EU/UK mark shares Class 9 as a number only (PPE ≠ software); an attorney clears that by comparing goods.
3. File Adiutor as a word mark in **Class 9 + 42** at USPTO and EUIPO early to establish priority.
4. Confirm nothing new is pending in Class 42 at USPTO/EUIPO (TMView lags recent filings by a few weeks).
5. Own the App Store name field and "Adiutor" Google SEO to out-rank `adiutor.co`.
6. Update product name from "Relay" to "Adiutor" across README, app metadata, and iOS bundle when the name is committed.
